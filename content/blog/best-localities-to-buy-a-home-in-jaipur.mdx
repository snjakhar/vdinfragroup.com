Ajmer Road is where we have built the most, and it is where we would start looking if we were buying today. The residential pockets just off the main road, Chordia City, Narayan Vihar, Kamala Nehru Nagar and Heerapura, are settled neighbourhoods with daily needs close by, while the road itself keeps the rest of the city within easy reach.

- **Healthcare:** Geetanjali Medical College and Hospital
- **Shopping:** D-Mart and Decathlon on Ajmer Road

## Mansarovar and Mansarovar Extension

Mansarovar is one of Jaipur's largest planned residential areas, with wide roads, parks and a metro line, so it is a name every buyer recognises and resale demand stays steady. Mansarovar Extension carries the same advantages further out, with newer homes and more space.

**What we have built here:** [Krishnam Kothi 94](/projects/krishnam-kothi-94) in Narayan Vihar, Mansarovar, and, under construction now, [Krishnam Kothi 176](/projects/krishnam-kothi-176), a luxury independent home in Vardhman Sarovar, Mansarovar Extension.

Jagatpura has grown quickly around its colleges and institutes, and its access to the airport makes it practical for people who travel often. It offers newer construction than the city's older neighbourhoods.

## How to choose

1. **Start with your daily routine.** Map the drive to work, school and family before you look at homes.
2. **Visit at different times of day.** Traffic and noise at 9 am can be very different from Sunday afternoon.
3. **Compare carpet area, not just super built-up area.** Ask for both and compare price per square foot on the same basis.
4. **Look at what the builder has already delivered nearby.** A completed building tells you more than any brochure.
5. **Decide whether you can wait.** A ready-to-move home lets you inspect exactly what you are buying and move in straight away; an under-construction home usually costs less but asks for patience. Our guide to [ready-to-move vs under-construction homes](/blog/ready-to-move-vs-under-construction) covers the trade-offs.
