Jaipur has grown well beyond the walled city. The metro, the ring road and steady demand from professionals, families and returning NRIs have created several strong residential corridors. Here is how the main ones compare.

## Vaishali Nagar and Gandhi Path

West Jaipur's most established address. Vaishali Nagar has mature markets, well-known schools and metro access, which keeps demand steady. Gandhi Path, just beyond it, offers newer projects and larger homes at a slightly lower price per square foot.

**Suits:** families who want an established neighbourhood with everything within a short drive.

## Mansarovar

One of the largest planned residential areas in the city, with wide roads, parks and a metro line. Prices are moderate, and resale demand is consistent because the area is so well known.

**Suits:** first-time buyers and families looking for value in a proven location.

## Malviya Nagar

Central, well connected to the airport and close to major malls and colleges. New supply is limited, so well-built projects here hold their value.

**Suits:** professionals who travel often and want a central home.

## Jagatpura

Jagatpura has become one of Jaipur's fastest-growing corridors, helped by its education hub and quick airport access. It offers a mix of apartments and villa communities at a range of budgets.

**Suits:** buyers looking for new construction and long-term appreciation.

## Ajmer Road

Ajmer Road is where Jaipur's villa communities and larger plotted developments are taking shape. Land is more available here, which means bigger homes, more open space and highway access.

**Suits:** villa buyers and those who want space and greenery.

## Tonk Road

Close to the airport and Sitapura's industrial and institutional cluster, Tonk Road is seeing new high-rise launches. Early buyers in upcoming projects here can benefit as the corridor matures.

**Suits:** investors and buyers comfortable with an under-construction purchase.

## How to choose

1. Start with your daily commute: office, schools and family.
2. Visit at different times of day to judge traffic and noise.
3. Compare price per square foot of **carpet area**, not super area.
4. Check the builder's track record in that locality.
